Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

100% On-Device Processing

LinkPresent is designed so confidential slide decks and webcam video never leave your device:

  • Camera video: Webcam frames are processed locally inside your browser or desktop app using self-hosted hand-tracking models. No video, images, or biometric data are transmitted to external servers.
  • Presentations (.pptx): Slide files loaded via drag-and-drop, the PowerPoint Add-in, or the Teams/Zoom Meeting Sidebar are unpacked locally in browser memory. No slide content is uploaded to our servers.
  • No tracking or telemetry: LinkPresent does not include third-party advertising trackers or background analytics telemetry.

Local Settings & Optional Diagnostics

Preferences such as layout mode and gesture toggles are stored locally in your browser's localStorage. Optional gesture recordings created via ?record=1 contain only 21 numeric hand-landmark coordinates per frame (never camera images) and stay on your local machine unless you explicitly share them.

Third-Party Host Platforms

When launched inside Microsoft PowerPoint, Microsoft Teams, or Zoom, those host applications operate under their own privacy policies and enterprise tenant controls.
